How I found
my way in

Origins

A B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, and then design took over. In 2020 I was designing daily social posts and print for Casper Academy, and teaching UI/UX to beginners on Superprof.

I learned in public: one Blender render a day for 50 days with zero 3D experience, a 100-day design challenge, and a stray dot in some CSS that cost me a ₹6,000 course. Lesson kept: only teach what you practise.

Read the dot story →

Going places

2021 took me to Hexnode in Kochi. Two years of the marketing website, A/B test pages, the full HexCon22 event site and its design system, and the brand video for MWC Barcelona 2023.

On the side, I kept teaching at wibe creative academy, and took on brand work like the Travel Memories identity.

The turning point

At CloudEagle.ai I went from designer to running design: a team of four, a design system before they joined, decks with the CEO, and around 80 blog posts a month with no AI image tools.

Then a hiring assignment at BiteSpeed turned into the job a week later. Now I design where brand meets performance: pages and ads with numbers attached.
