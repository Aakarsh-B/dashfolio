    Good to know

    How the colours are picked

    The image is shrunk, then grouped into clusters of similar colours (k-means, in a colour space that matches how we see). The biggest groups come first.

    Pick the exact pixel

    Click or tap the image to sample one spot. The picked colour can be copied or added to the palette.

    Moodboards work best

    A screenshot of a moodboard or a brand photo gives a more usable palette than a busy scene with many objects.

    Private by design

    Your image never leaves your device. Everything happens in your browser.
