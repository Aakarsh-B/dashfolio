Good to know

 
Lock, then explore
Start with your brand colour, lock it, and generate. Each round picks a colour scheme (close neighbours, opposites or triads) built around what you've locked.

 
Even steps, not guesses
Scales are made in OKLCH, a colour space where equal steps look equally different to the eye, so the middle shades don't turn muddy.

 
Your colours stay put
Each scale is built around your exact colour: it lands on the step that matches its lightness, and the others are spaced evenly around it.

 
One download for everything
CSS variables, a Tailwind block or JSON design tokens for every scale at once, or a PNG sheet for Figma and brand decks.
