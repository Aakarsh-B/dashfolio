Brand revamp for BiteSpeed

Brand identity

A modern, AI-native brand direction for BiteSpeed, an AI marketing and support platform for Shopify and e-commerce brands.

View on Behance ↗
Role
Brand designer
Tools
Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop
Deliverables
Brand direction, Colour system, Typography, Social carousel, Product feature banner

The challenge

This started as the design assignment in BiteSpeed's hiring process: propose a revamped brand. BiteSpeed helps e-commerce brands run marketing and support across WhatsApp, email and chat, increasingly with AI doing the work. The brand needed to feel as current as the product: trustworthy enough for businesses handing customer conversations to automation, and distinctive in a crowded SaaS space.

Approach

  1. Two directionsI built two complete identity directions to compare; this is one of them.
  2. Colour with a reasonBlue for trust, intelligence and reliability; purple hints for creativity and forward thinking. Five colours: #044DFA, #00FDFD, #A89ED4, #0A0A0A and #FCFCFC.
  3. One flexible typefaceAcumin VF: modern, legible and variable, so the same family works from product UI to ads.
  4. Show it workingPut the system into real formats: a brand board, a three-slide social carousel and a feature banner for SmartReach AI.

The solution

Blue-to-purple gradients and abstract shapes signal innovation, and smooth gradients with clean typography keep it sophisticated rather than loud.

  • A brand board with logo, palette, headline style and social proof in one view
  • A carousel that moves from "AI is redefining e-commerce" to four ways AI helps, to an install call to action
  • A feature banner pairing four benefits with a real WhatsApp conversation flow

Result

A week after submitting it, I joined BiteSpeed as Brand Designer.
