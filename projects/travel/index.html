Travel Memories

Brand identity revampPublished Jun 2023

Logo mark, colour and applications for a travel brand: a map pin with a plane cut through it.

View on Behance ↗
Role
Brand designer
Tools
Illustrator
Deliverables
Logo mark, Logotype, Colour palette, Logo versions, Applications

The challenge

A complete brand identity revamp for Travel Memories, a travel company based abroad. The brand needed a mark that says "travel" instantly and still works small, in one colour, and on merchandise.

Approach

  1. One ideaPut the two things travel is about, the place and the journey, into one mark: a map pin with a plane cut through it.
  2. A construction gridDraw the mark on overlapping circles so the curves stay balanced and it holds up small.
  3. A small paletteThree colours: green #5EBF88, blue #0768B3 and white.
  4. Every versionColour, light and white versions, checked on navy, blue and white, then on merchandise.

The solution

A map pin with a plane cut through it, drawn on a circle-based construction grid so the curves stay balanced. A three-colour palette (green #5EBF88, blue #0768B3 and white) gives colour, light and reversed versions that hold up on navy, blue or white. The system is shown applied to a tote bag, t-shirt and business cards.
