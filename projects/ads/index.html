Marketing design

Performance ad creatives

Performance-focused ad creatives for e-commerce and education brands, built to stop the scroll and get the click.

View on Dribbble ↗
Role
Concept and visual design
Tools
Figma, Photoshop
Deliverables
Meta ads, Google ads, Variations for A/B tests

The challenge

Paid ads have a second to land in a crowded feed. They need to stop the scroll, explain the benefit fast and get the click, and they have to be easy to test.

Approach

  1. Lead with the problemBold, problem-led headlines, like "From 2 days to 2 hours", that say the benefit before anything else.
  2. Quick-scan proofBenefits in a few words each, so the ad reads in a glance.
  3. CTA where the eye landsContrast moves attention from hook to proof to action.
  4. Modular by designLayouts that swap easily into new sizes and variations for A/B testing.

The solution

Ad sets across Meta and Google that explore different directions, campaign concepts and formats while staying on-brand. Designed for conversion, not just looks.
