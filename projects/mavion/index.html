Mavion

Brand identity · Website

Brand identity and website for Mavion IT Solutions, a software and hardware sales company, set up from the ground up.

Visit live site ↗View on Dribbble ↗
Role
Brand designer and web developer
Deliverables
Logo, Brand direction, Website design, Website build

The challenge

Mavion brings 20+ years of experience in software and hardware sales into its own business, founded and led by a woman entrepreneur. It needed its whole visual side built from scratch: an identity that feels confident and technical but approachable, and a website that explains a wide range of products and services.

Approach

  1. Logo and directionA geometric M mark and clean wordmark, in an electric blue that says technology, reliability and momentum.
  2. Website structureOrganise a wide catalogue (software, business hardware, peripherals, support, BIM and web services) and the industries Mavion serves around one clear path to get in touch.
  3. Design and buildDesigned the site, then built it, including the CSS, with a little help from code.

The solution

A bold, consistent identity that works across every digital touchpoint, and a live website the team can point customers to. In short, I set up the visual side of the business from the ground up.

Result

Live at mavion.in.
