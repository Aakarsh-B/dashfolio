Creativate

Website design

A clean, conversion-focused website for Creativate, a creative agency covering branding, UI/UX, web development and social media.

Visit live site ↗View on Dribbble ↗
Role
Lead designer and developer
Timeline
Aug – Nov 2023
Deliverables
Site architecture, Landing page, Inner pages, Social creative

The challenge

Before the revamp, Creativate had five problems: low form submissions from unclear CTAs and missing trust signals, low credibility because past work wasn't showcased, visitors leaving after one page, a generic brand perception, and a poor mobile experience that hurt conversions.

Approach

  1. Architecture firstHome leads to Work, Services, Blog and Contact, with Work and Blog inner pages, and every path ends at "Book free consultation".
  2. Proof up frontProject showcases and client logos in the hero, so prospects see the work the moment they land.
  3. Explain the processA clear three-step process (Connect, Analyse, Get Started) to make working together feel transparent.
  4. Make the form easyOnly the fields that matter, built responsive first, because lead generation was the main pain point.

The solution

A dark, confident site that sets Creativate apart from other agencies, set in Poppins and Glacial Indifference with a #0067FF primary blue.

  • A homepage contact form that makes a young company feel approachable and opens more chances for leads
  • A consultation form that asks for the service, budget and a short summary, with business name and website optional
  • Pages for work, services, FAQs and privacy, laid out to work properly on mobile
  • Matching social creative: FAQ posts, a hiring post and an academy launch

Result

Better UX flow, visual consistency and mobile responsiveness, with more engagement and form submissions from clearer layouts, CTAs and faster pages. Live at creativate.in.
